Bells and Whistles adds rolling-stock features to Railroader: tender bells, flexible hoses and pipes, driven animations, horn/whistle selection and auxiliary water tenders. The combined 0.3.0 release also brings together geared locomotive behavior, oil and wood firing, fuel services and Relay APIs for content authors.

Players install it for content packs that use these features.

:::note[Version and compatibility]

**These are the development docs for Bells and Whistles 0.3.0.** Choose the matching frozen release in the version menu. The maintainer has confirmed earlier in-game testing of 0.3.0. The combined geared, firing, fuel-service and Relay artifact has additional offline/native fixture qualification; fresh combined gameplay acceptance remains separate.

The combined package is being prepared for **Railroader 0.6.2 or later**, using the game's native mod system.

If you play Railroader 2025.1, this version isn't for you. Bells and Whistles 0.3.0 has no Unity Mod Manager build.

[See what changed in 0.3.0](/BellsAndWhistles-Docs/whats-new/) or choose an older release from the version selector.

:::

Bells, connectors and driven animations provide sound or visuals. Auxiliary tenders move water; the geared, firing and service integrations also affect gameplay. The combined B&W package participates in multiplayer package/version matching.

Features use the declarations and prerequisites in their guides. Tender bells can follow existing Bell components automatically, and horn/whistle selection applies to locomotives with both instruments.
