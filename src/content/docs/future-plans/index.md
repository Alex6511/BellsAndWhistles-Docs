:::caution[Always subject to change]

**All future plans are subject to change.** Scope, behavior, configuration, tool interfaces, release targets, and timing may change after review or implementation. A target version is a plan, not a release commitment.

These pages describe proposals for modders to review. Their examples are not installation instructions for an available feature, and publication here does not establish implementation or runtime acceptance.

:::

## Proposals open for review

| Proposal | Planned B&W version | What modders can review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Valve gear authoring and ADK preview](/BellsAndWhistles-Docs/future-plans/valve-gear/) | **0.4.0**, subject to change | Preparing forward, neutral, and reverse cycles; conventional and articulated setup; preview controls; export and fallback behavior. |

The target B&W version is separate from the Railroader and Unity versions a proposal expects to support. Each proposal states those targets and its validation limits.

## How to give feedback

Read the proposed authoring steps with your own model or workflow in mind. [Open an issue in the documentation repository](https://github.com/Alex6511/BellsAndWhistles-Docs/issues/new) and link the proposal. Describe the step or example that needs changing and, where useful, the mechanism, animation software, or hierarchy involved. Share only assets and screenshots you have permission to publish.

## How proposals become release documentation

Future plans live only in the development documentation and are excluded from release snapshots. Once a feature is implemented and its supported behavior is verified, its accepted authoring guide can be moved into the appropriate release documentation. The [versioning rules](/BellsAndWhistles-Docs/versions/) still apply; existing release archives are preserved.
